# Valley run (assets/levels/valley.msl), with real controls: fly up the valley,
# land on the pad by the base, step out, walk in, shoot the guard, hold G on the
# console to drop the force field, walk back with a jetpack hop, board and fly
# through to the end. The ship is 5x stock (60 m/s flat out); the pilot runs at
# 6 m/s. Mouse: 714 counts is a quarter turn. Ends in "final phase Won".
#
#   cargo run --release -p miniscent-game -- --level assets/levels/valley.msl \
#       --script apps/game/scripts/valley.txt --screenshot target/shots/valley.png \
#       --frames 3000 --shots-at 5,10.8,11.4,14.5,19,25,33.6,35.8,41

# The flight in: up the valley, sidestep onto the pad's line, drop onto it.
at 0.5s hold W for 8.08s
at 6.0s hold A for 0.4s
at 9.5s hold F for 0.8s
# Out of the ship, turn east to the base door and walk in.
at 11.0s tap B
at 11.6s mouse 714 0 over 0.3s
at 12.2s hold W for 4.6s
# The guard in the west hall.
at 17.0s mouse -526 0 over 0.3s
at 17.4s fire for 1.5s
# Through the inner doorway to the console, and hold the use key on it.
at 19.2s mouse 526 0 over 0.3s
at 19.6s hold W for 3.5s
at 23.6s hold G for 2.4s
# Back out to the ship, with a jetpack hop on the way.
at 26.3s mouse 1428 0 over 0.5s
at 27.0s hold W for 8.1s
at 33.0s hold Space for 1.0s
# Aboard, lift off and fly on through where the field was.
at 35.4s tap B
at 35.9s hold R for 0.6s
at 36.7s hold W for 9.5s
at 47s quit
