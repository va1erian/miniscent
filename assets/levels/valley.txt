# Valley run: the ship and the pilot in one level. Fly north up a long valley; a
# force field closes it at y = 600. Land on the pad beside the enemy base, step
# out (B), walk in through a door too small for the ship, hold G on the console
# to drop the field, walk back, board (B) and fly on to the end of the valley.
# Wiring is in valley.st; valley.test.st plays it through.
#
# Scale: units are the pilot's metres. The ship is 5 times its stock size (the
# scale line): a 2.5 m sphere, about 8 m nose to tail, 60 m/s flat out. The
# base door is 3 m wide and 2.5 m high: the pilot (1.8 by 0.7 m) walks in, the
# ship (a 5 m opening at least) cannot. `miniscent --scale-guide 5` prints the full table.
#
# Pack it after an edit:
#   cargo run --release -p miniscent-game -- --pack-level assets/levels/valley.txt assets/levels/valley.msl

# The play space: every wall is the night sky (material 10, stars: self-lit, so it
# costs no light).

# its door on the west side facing the pad. The floor is a step up from the ground.
